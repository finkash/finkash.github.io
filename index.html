Who I Am

I finished high school at the Jane Sandanski Gymnasium in Strumica with excellent success, and later graduated in computer science in Skopje. Tech taught me how to think, but everything else taught me how to live.

What Drives Me

I’m drawn to things that require patience and attention:

  • Photography, because it forces you to slow down and really look at the world.
  • Books and theater, because they capture emotions words can't always reach.
  • Formula 1, because there’s no better lesson in strategy, pressure, and resilience.

I like the space where logic and creativity meet. That’s where I feel most at home.

A Few Things I Believe

  • Success comes slowly, then all at once. Be patient with the slow part.
  • Happiness is almost never where you expect it; it shows up in small things—a good sentence, a quiet morning, or music that catches you off guard.
  • Find at least one thing you follow with unreasonable passion—it keeps you human.
